group_chat_v1.txt  (MemeWorld; adapted from GA v3_ChatGPT/iterative_convo_v1.txt for three or more people)

Changes from upstream: the speaker talks to the whole table instead of one target person, every
participant is named, and the output keys are fixed ("utterance", "end") instead of being keyed by
the persona's name.
