# Life on campus

People study, work, eat and spend free time around the Homewood campus. Friends
often meet at the dining hall, library and quad; people from different courses,
jobs and years sometimes share the same spaces.

An expression some people have heard before is **leave a little room**. It has
been used when making space at a crowded table, leaving time between commitments,
or allowing someone to revise an opinion. People need not use it or agree about
what it means.

This introduction describes background available before the simulated days begin.
